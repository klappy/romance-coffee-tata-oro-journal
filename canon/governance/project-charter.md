# Project Charter — Romance by Tata Oro

> This repository is the canonical governance baseline for the Romance by Tata Oro project. All future sessions MUST fetch project governance live from this repo via oddkit (`knowledge_base_url: https://github.com/klappy/romance-coffee-tata-oro-journal`) at the moment of use — never from model memory. Access is not enforcement; fetching is.

## Summary

Romance by Tata Oro is a premium Colombian coffee brand co-launched by Klappy (Chris) and Tatiana (Tata Oro), extending her established beauty and hair care brand. The product is a 70/30 blend of Castillo Chocolate and Bourbon Rosado Canela from La Karmela / 930 Colombian Coffee SAS (Bogotá). Positioning: "a special cup of coffee for everyday people." Origin: the daily ritual of fresh-ground cinnamon bark in Tatiana's morning coffee, sustained for over 18 months.

## Roles & Decision Authority

| Role | Person | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Captain / project lead | Klappy (Chris) | Final call on strategy, briefs, encode/canon ratification |
| Brand face & voice | Tatiana (Tata Oro) | Final word on anything in her authorial voice; brand-feel veto |
| Importer / roaster / pricing | Elkin Gamba (930 / La Karmela, Bogotá + South Florida) | Supply, pricing, production, logistics |
| Master roaster | Daniel Giraldo Vélez | Roast execution |
| Design & marketing | Andrea, Gustavo, Laura (Colombia) | Packaging design, marketing assets |
| Original designer | Tatiana's designer | Romance brand book author |

Rules of authority:

1. Nothing in Tatiana's authorial voice (captions, Stories, letters signed by her) commits without her review of the exact text.
2. Product decisions MUST re-clear the three-constraint architecture (see `brand-constraints.md`) before changing.
3. Decisions, corrections, and learnings are encoded via oddkit and persisted to this repo's `journal/` — encode output does not persist itself.

## Repo-as-Baseline Rule

- Future sessions ("projections") pass `knowledge_base_url=https://github.com/klappy/romance-coffee-tata-oro-journal` to oddkit `search` / `get` / `catalog` / `encode` so retrieval operates under this overlay.
- Governance lives here, in versioned files — not in any model's memory of it. Memory may summarize; canon decides.
- When canon and memory conflict, canon wins. When canon and a newer ratified decision conflict, encode the supersession and update canon in the same session.

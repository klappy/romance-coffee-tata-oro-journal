# Brand Constraints

> Binding constraints on all product, pricing, packaging, and positioning decisions. Any proposal that violates one of these MUST be challenged (oddkit challenge) and ratified by the captain — and by Tatiana where her voice or brand feel is touched — before it proceeds.

## The Three-Constraint Architecture (binding)

Every product decision MUST simultaneously satisfy:

1. **Tatiana's preference** — Castillo as the base she loves.
2. **The audience price ceiling** — ~$25 retail / 12 oz. Bourbon Rosado Canela solo is unattainable (~$40 retail) and MUST NOT be the sole SKU.
3. **The daily ritual** — the cinnamon character of Rosado Canela, mirroring the 18+ month ritual of fresh-ground cinnamon bark.

The 70/30 Castillo Chocolate / Bourbon Rosado Canela blend is the only configuration satisfying all three at once. This is the blend's strategic defensibility: constraint-driven, not preference-driven. Any future product change MUST re-clear all three constraints. Fallback if blending is ever unavailable at scale: Castillo Chocolate solo, with Rosado held for a future seasonal SKU.

Provenance: removed from `cafe.tataoro.com` in the 2026-07-31 site rebuild (deploy sanity checks required both language versions GONE); carried as a standing constraint in the 2026-07-31 NotebookLM package charter; re-affirmed by the captain in-session 2026-08-01 after the retired line leaked back into draft copy via this document's stale text.

  - **"Two stories. One cup."** / **"Dos historias. Una taza."**

## Banned Phrases (binding)

The following MUST NOT appear in any external or generated output, in any language or close paraphrase. Copy work MUST grep its output against this list before presenting. When a live surface (site, Shopify, packaging) is found carrying a banned phrase, that is a defect to fix, not a precedent to cite.

1. **"A special cup of coffee for everyday people"** / **"un café especial para la gente de todos los días"** — retired positioning line (see retirement notice above).
2. **"Coming soon"** / **"muy pronto"** — no future-availability promises anywhere; state only what is purchasable now (captain ruling 2026-08-01).
3. **"Approachable luxury"** — internal strategic shorthand only, never external.
4. **"Tata Oro Cafe"** as a product rendering — the product is **Romance by Tata Oro**; the storefront is **Café Tata Oro**.
5. **"Piloncillo"** — Colombian Spanish only: **panela**.

## Visual Identity Locks (binding)

- Brand name: **Romance by Tata Oro** (not "Tata Oro Cafe").
- Cursive gold "Romance" script lettering: locked, non-negotiable.
- Gold-on-black color DNA carried from Tata Oro (palette: Irish Coffee, Butterscotch Mousse, Cocoa Cream, Gold).
- Packaging: matte black bag, strong negative space, minimal design.
- Core illustration: coffee steam transforming into embracing couple silhouettes; the negative space between figures implies a heart. A literal heart graphic MUST NOT be used.

## Format & Pricing (current ratified state)

- Single SKU. Production split 50/50 ground / whole bean (revised 2026-06-02 from the poll-informed ground-only spec; personal-consumption freshness shifted the split).
- Ground bags MUST have degassing valves.
- Retail target ~$25 / 12 oz at ~3x markup; wholesale all-in $9.27/bag per accepted La Karmela quote.

## The Story (documentary, not marketing)

The origin story is factual and MUST be told as documentary: "algún día" cartoons while dating → real daily ritual of chocolate-note coffee + fresh-ground cinnamon bark + panela after marriage. The blend recreates that cup, grown near Tatiana's hometown. Embellishment beyond the facts is out of bounds.

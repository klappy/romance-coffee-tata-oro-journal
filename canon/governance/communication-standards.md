# Communication Standards

> Binding standards for all external communications: partner emails, executive briefs, social content, and packaging copy.

## Bilingual Rule (binding)

- All external materials MUST be produced in both English and Spanish.
- Spanish MUST follow Colombian conventions: **panela**, never piloncillo. When in doubt, prefer the Colombian term.
- Social content for Tatiana's audience leads in Spanish with English in parallel.

## Authorial-Voice Review Rule (binding)

- Nothing in Tatiana's authorial voice — captions, Stories copy, letters or emails signed by her — MAY be sent or published without her review of the exact final text. Drafts are drafts until she has seen the words that ship.

## Tone with Colombian Partners (binding)

- Communications with Elkin and the Colombian team MUST carry relational warmth and directness without being commanding — appropriate to the cultural and pastoral context.
- Canonical example of the corrected register: "We need to make sure we understand the timeline and milestones to launch our brand as soon as possible" (replacing an earlier commanding draft).
- Deadlines MUST be stated precisely ("end of May", not "before June").

## Ask-First Brief Structure (binding)

Executive briefs to partners MUST follow this order:

1. **What we want to launch** (the ask leads)
2. **What we found** (the discovery, celebrated — e.g., all four samples honored)
3. **Is the capability available?** (explicit question — e.g., "is custom blending available at 100-bag scale?")
4. **The fallback** (named — e.g., Castillo Chocolate solo)

Briefs MUST NOT presume a partner capability exists. The assumption-led brief is a known failure mode, corrected by Tatiana on 2026-05-26 when a draft presumed custom blending instead of asking. The executive shape is ask-leads, not discovery-narrative-leads.

## Social Content Principles

- Couple-together content (Chris + Tatiana around coffee) outperforms baseline solo content — build content arcs from the couple dynamic; it IS the brand story.
- Polls are designed for behavior, not preference ("what do you actually use?"), with parenthetical explanations to prevent aspirational answers.
- Pre-launch content MUST NOT commit to a launch date until manufacturing dates are confirmed.

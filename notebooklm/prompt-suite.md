## Scene 1 — Emigration / What You Carry

**Shot description:** Close on hands packing a small woven or leather
bag by window light — folded fabric, a photograph, then a small paper
sack of green/roasted coffee beans placed in last, deliberately. Cut to
a wide of a window looking out on a soft, unplaceable golden-hour
exterior (not literally an airport — evocative, not literal).

**Palette/light:** Ink Black interior falling to Champagne Gold window
light; Irish Coffee shadow fill on the bag's leather. No blue or cool
tones.

**Motion:** Slow push-in on the hands; a single unhurried gesture
placing the coffee sack. No handheld shake — steady, deliberate,
documentary stillness.

**Duration:** ~8–10s.

**Voiceover timing (ES leads):** «Dicen que cuando emigras dejas muchas
cosas atrás — pero también llevas contigo una parte de quien eres. En mi
caso, fue el café.» (EN echo plays underneath/after, quieter per the
EN/ES rhythm rule — "They say that when you emigrate, you leave many
things behind... For me, it was coffee.")

**EN/ES caption hook:** ES on screen: "Dicen que cuando emigras dejas
muchas cosas atrás." EN caption (secondary, smaller): "What you carry."

## Scene 2 — Morning Ritual (Two Cups, Almost Two Years)

**Shot description:** A kitchen counter at dawn. Two cups, side by side.
A hand (Chris's, implied not literal casting direction) pouring one cup
first, then the second — the everyday choreography of a ritual repeated
almost two years running, even a suitcase or travel mug visible in one
cutaway to imply "even from the road."

**Palette/light:** Butterscotch warm mid-tones on wood counter, Ink
Black background falling off past the counter edge, Champagne Gold rim
light on the cup rims and steam.

**Motion:** Slow, real-time pour; steam rising and catching light; a
soft rack-focus from the pour to a wedding ring on the hand (documentary
detail, not staged jewelry glamour).

**Duration:** ~10–12s.

**Voiceover timing:** «Con el tiempo encontré el amor, construí un
hogar, una familia... Cada mañana, durante casi dos años de matrimonio —
incluso de viaje — Chris ha preparado el café de Tatiana.» EN echo:
"Every morning for almost two years of marriage — even from the road —
Chris has made Tatiana's coffee."

**EN/ES caption hook:** ES: "Cada mañana, casi dos años." EN: "Every
morning, for almost two years."

## Scene 3 — Roaster Beat (Chocolate — Panela — Canela)

**Shot description:** Macro/tabletop shots: roasted beans catching gold
light (Castillo, deep dark chocolate-brown beans), then fresh cinnamon
bark being ground or shaved (Bourbon Rosado's canela note), then a small
block or shaving of panela. Three ingredients, three quick but
unhurried macro beats, on a dark wood or burlap surface.

**Palette/light:** Irish Coffee and Espresso browns dominate; Deep Gold
highlight on bean surfaces and cinnamon bark; burlap texture visible per
brand-guide §9 imagery direction.

**Motion:** Slow macro push-ins on each ingredient in turn; light catch
on bean oil sheen; cinnamon curl falling in slow motion.

**Duration:** ~8–10s.

**Voiceover timing:** «...chocolate, panela y canela recién molida. Los
chocolates son el regalo del romance; la canela, su calidez.» EN echo:
"Chocolate, panela, fresh-ground cinnamon. Chocolates are the gift of
romance; cinnamon is its warmth."

**EN/ES caption hook:** ES: "Chocolate, panela y canela." EN: "Chocolate.
Panela. Cinnamon."

**Sensory reference (not literal on-screen text):** may draw texture
inspiration from `notebooklm/sources/blind-tasting-provenance.md`
aroma language (Castillo "dark chocolate," "leather"; Rosado "cinnamon
sticks," "smells like Christmas") for mood only — do not caption these
words verbatim on screen; they are sensory-note provenance, not approved
copy.

## Scene 4 — The Pour ("Drinks Like Cream" — Visual Metaphor Only)

**Shot description:** A slow pour into a cup, viewed close, showing the
coffee's body and sheen — swirl, viscosity, the way light plays across
the surface as it settles. The visual goal is to evoke smoothness and
body through the coffee's OWN color and motion.

> **BINDING: NO literal cream, milk, dairy, or any white liquid may
> appear in this shot or any cutaway within it — not poured in, not
> pre-mixed, not implied by a creamer container in frame.** "Drinks like
> cream" is Tatiana's own metaphor for mouthfeel
> (`notebooklm/sources/blind-tasting-provenance.md`), rendered here
> purely through the coffee's own motion, gloss, and light — never
> through dairy imagery. If a generated draft shows anything
> cream-colored entering the cup, reject and regenerate.

**Palette/light:** Ink Black cup and background, Champagne Gold
highlight tracing the pour arc and the settling swirl, Cocoa Cream only
as a caption-text color — never as an on-screen liquid.

**Motion:** Slow-motion pour (documentary slow-pour pacing, not a
stylized bullet-time effect); gentle settle and a single lazy steam
curl.

**Duration:** ~6–8s.

**Voiceover timing:** Plays under the blend-block beat, e.g. "A
delicate effervescence that drinks like cream, with a bright zing of
canela" / «como crema en el café, sin nada de crema» — spoken, never
shown literally.

**EN/ES caption hook:** ES: "Como crema en el café, sin nada de crema."
EN: "Like cream in coffee, without any cream." (Caption text is the only
place "cream" appears — never the image.)

## Scene 5 — Bag Reveal

**Shot description:** The matte black 12 oz stand-up pouch, front then
back, on dark wood with scattered roasted beans — direct visual
reference: the two live product photos at
`notebooklm/sources/shopify-product-facts.md` (front: bag with gold
"Romance" cursive script and botanical line-art; back: blend/origin/
roast/grind label per `notebooklm/sources/label-romance-by-tata-oro.md`).
Slow reveal turn from front to back, or a match-cut between the two
stills.

**Palette/light:** Matches the reference photos exactly — Ink Black bag
field, Champagne Gold script, Deep Gold botanical line-art, soft
directional light, no clinical/bright product-shot lighting.

**Motion:** Slow rotate or dolly reveal; steam wisp optional in
foreground for continuity with Scenes 2–4; no spinning-product-ad
motion.

**Duration:** ~6–8s.

**Voiceover timing:** Plays under "Medium roast · 12 oz / 340 g · Hand-
picked, small-lot" and the origin lines (Castillo Lavado · Cundinamarca;
Bourbon Rosado · Huila) — specialty facts, spoken in EN per the
EN/ES-rhythm rule (English carries information).

**EN/ES caption hook:** ES: "Café de especialidad. Lote pequeño." EN:
"Specialty. Hand-picked. Small-lot." (Specialty-before-price doctrine:
no price or buy CTA appears in this scene or anywhere before it.)

## Scene 6 — Story-Card / QR Close

**Shot description:** A printed story/insert card on the same dark wood
surface, held or laid flat, showing a QR code corner and short story
text; final frame holds on the gold "Romance" wordmark and the closing
tagline, then fades to Ink Black.

**Palette/light:** Espresso brown text on Cocoa Cream card stock (per
style bible), Ink Black final hold frame, Champagne Gold wordmark.

**Motion:** Static hold on the card (2–3s), then a slow fade of the
wordmark and tagline over black. No motion after the tagline lands —
let it sit.

**Duration:** ~6–8s.

**Voiceover timing:** Closing line only — «Dos historias. Una taza.» /
"Two stories. One cup." Rendered as its own held sentence-unit beat per
the Tagline rule (charter constraint #4) — never split across the card
shot and the wordmark fade.

**EN/ES caption hook:** ES: "Dos historias. Una taza." EN: "Two stories.
One cup." Both rendered as complete, unbroken lines — never wrapped
mid-sentence.

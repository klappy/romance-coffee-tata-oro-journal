## Observations for later reconciliation (flag, not ruling)

1. **Component naming vs canon.** The label says **"Castillo Lavado"** and **"Bourbon Rosado"**; `brand-constraints.md` records the blend as **"Castillo Chocolate"** + **"Bourbon Rosado Canela"** (70/30). "Lavado" (washed) is a process descriptor and "Chocolate"/"Canela" are tasting-note descriptors — likely the same beans described in different language, but confirm before either is treated as authoritative.
2. **Origin wording.** The label attributes Castillo to **Cundinamarca** and Bourbon Rosado to **Huila**; the bag mockup back reads **"Guayabal de Síquima, Huila, Colombia."** Guayabal de Síquima and Huila appearing together is worth verifying — confirm the correct department per component.
3. **Blend ratio (70/30) is not printed** on the label or mockup.
4. **Marketing taglines.** "TWO STORIES. ONE CUP." and "A Colombian soul. An American spirit. One beautiful blend." are additional lines alongside the canon positioning line "a special cup of coffee for everyday people." Not a conflict — complementary — but all audience-facing copy is subject to Tatiana's authorial-voice / brand-feel review.
5. **Usage.** These assets are earmarked as source material and inspiration for `cafe.tataoro.com` website assets.

# Charter — NotebookLM Package: Context + Prompts for Cinematic Video

**Filed:** 2026-07-31 (EDT) / 2026-08-01T03:13Z · **Workstream:** romance-coffee
**Principal:** captain · **Seat:** Otto (CDO) · **Status:** OPEN — next session boards on this

## Mission

Assemble a NotebookLM-ready package — a curated context corpus plus a
cinematic prompt library — so that captain (or any fresh session) can
generate storyboards, shot lists, and video-model prompts for Romance by
Tata Oro without re-deriving an ounce of context. The package IS the
expectation-management artifact: the video tools inherit the same canon
the humans hold.

## Deliverables

**D1 — Source pack + manifest.** Export-ready copies (not just pointers)
of: brand guide sections (story, palette, voice), brand constraints,
Tatiana's ratified voiceover script (ES primary + EN), tagline, per-bean
flavor notes, product description (EN + registered ES), Instagram caption
+ bio (ratified originals), cafe.tataoro.com copy, product facts (SKUs,
price, photos, GIDs), and the blind-tasting provenance quotes (Bee conv
8289557 / daily 663491, conv 8123017 for Rosado apple-pie/cinnamon +
Castillo chocolate). Manifest lists each file, its source of record, and
its ratification status.

**D2 — Style bible for motion.** Matte black / gold / cream / cocoa;
moody café morning light; serif elegance; slow pour pacing; specialty-
before-price doctrine applied to video (the viewer knows it's specialty
before any commerce beat); EN/ES bilingual rhythm (Spanish leads emotion,
English carries information).

**D3 — Prompt suite.** Scene-level cinematic prompts (Veo/Sora/Runway-
class) mapped to the story beats: emigration/what-you-carry, the morning
ritual (two cups, almost two years), roaster beat (chocolate — panela —
canela), the pour ("drinks like cream" — visual metaphor, NO actual
cream on screen), bag reveal, story-card/QR close. Each prompt carries:
shot description, palette/light notes, motion, duration, voiceover
timing against her ratified script, EN/ES caption hook.

**D4 — NotebookLM assembly instructions.** Upload order, notebook
structure, and the meta-prompts to run inside NotebookLM (generate
storyboard from beats; audio overview as trailer test; Q&A grounding
checks).

## Constraints (binding)

- Tatiana gate on ALL customer-facing output; captain review for his
  authorial voice. The package produces drafts, never publishes.
- Documentary honesty: every claim traces to a pack source. The cream
  line is HER QUOTE — render as metaphor, never literal cream.
- The retired positioning line ("a special cup of coffee for everyday
  people") MUST NOT appear — canon amendment still pending, so the line
  will keep resurfacing from stale canon; the pack must carry the
  retirement notice explicitly.
- Tagline renders as sentence-unit lines, never broken mid-sentence.

## Acceptance

A cold session (or captain alone) imports the package into NotebookLM in
under ten minutes and produces a first storyboard with zero context
questions. Every generated claim is traceable to a manifest source.

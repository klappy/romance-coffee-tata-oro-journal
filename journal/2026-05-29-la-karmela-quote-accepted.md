# Session — La Karmela Quote Received & Accepted (2026-05-29)

> Quote from La Karmela / 930 Café received: $931 total for 100 bags of exact 70/30 Castillo Chocolate + Bourbon Rosado Canela blend. Free valve bags and stickers. Quote accepted. Internal-success-first constraint articulated.

## Observations

- **Quote details:** 100 × 12 oz bags, 70/30 blend confirmed exactly, US$931 total ($9.27/bag all-in)
- **Packaging at $0:** Valve bags + "Romance" logo sticker + back info sticker — all free (strong relational signal from La Karmela)
- **Shipping:** 2 × 34 kg boxes = $4. DHL air paid by client.
- **Terms:** 100% advance payment. Production 5 days + DHL transport 4 days.
- **Origin:** La Karmela warehouse, Bogotá D.C., Colombia

## Decisions

- **Accept the quote in full** — price within target wholesale range for $25 retail
- **100-bag personal baseline secured:** Ritual coffee for Tatiana guaranteed for full year at higher quality and lower effective cost than prior subscriptions

## Learnings

- **Pricing is competitive:** Pure Pink Bourbon / Rosado 12 oz bags retail $23–$30+ in specialty market. The 70/30 at ~$25 is well-positioned.
- **The story is the differentiator:** The lived ritual ("algún día" cartoons → daily coffee with ground cinnamon) is genuine, ownable, and unreplicable. Documentary testimony, not generic romance marketing.
- **La Karmela partnership quality** (exact blend match + $0 packaging) strengthens foundation for both personal use and any future DTC.

## Constraints

- **Internal success first:** The product succeeds the moment the first bag delivers the exact chocolate + cinnamon ritual coffee to Tatiana every morning. External sales, while welcome, are secondary. The 100-bag buffer guarantees this outcome regardless of outside demand.

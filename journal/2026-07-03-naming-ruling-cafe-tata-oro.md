# Captain's Naming Ruling — Café Tata Oro (house) / Romance by Tata Oro (product) (2026-07-03)

> Following the blind naming test and its 50/50 US-market re-analysis, the captain ruled. The tension between the blind-test winner ("Café Tata Oro") and the canon-locked brand name ("Romance by Tata Oro") is resolved by **separating layers**: **Café Tata Oro** becomes the house / account / storefront brand (`@cafe.tataoro`, `cafe.tataoro.com`), and **Romance by Tata Oro** remains the **product** — the first blend — preserving the locked visual identity. The empirical winner is adopted where it is strongest (the audience-facing shopfront); the locked name is preserved where it belongs (the SKU).

## Brand architecture (ratified)

| Layer | Name | Identifiers |
|---|---|---|
| **House / account / storefront** | **Café Tata Oro** | handle `@cafe.tataoro` · website `cafe.tataoro.com` |
| **Product (first blend / SKU)** | **Romance by Tata Oro** | canon-locked visual identity — gold cursive "Romance" script |

Note on the handle: the blind test presented `@cafetataoro` (no dot); the ruled handle is `@cafe.tataoro` (dotted), matching the project's dotted-handle convention. The dotted form also tested best on trust with warm English followers in the re-analysis.

## Decisions

1. **[D] Two-tier brand architecture ratified (captain's ruling):** house/account brand = **Café Tata Oro** (`@cafe.tataoro`, `cafe.tataoro.com`); product/first blend = **Romance by Tata Oro** (locked visual identity). The Café-Tata-Oro-vs-lock tension is resolved by separating shopfront from blend, not by overriding the lock. The blind-test winner is adopted as the house brand; the canon-locked name is preserved as the product name.

## Constraints

2. **[C] Canon follow-up required:** `brand-constraints.md` currently states a single flat lock — "Brand name: Romance by Tata Oro (not 'Tata Oro Cafe')." The ruling introduces a house/product split in which the house account **is** "Café Tata Oro." `brand-constraints.md` must be updated to encode the two-tier architecture. Because the visual-identity lock is **brand-feel territory, this canon edit is gated on Tatiana's brand-feel review** before it is ratified into the file (project-charter rule: changes touching Tatiana's brand feel require her sign-off).

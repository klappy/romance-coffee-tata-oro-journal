## Amazon distributor model (friends listing on Amazon)

Design principle (internal only): split the true profit pool 50/50.
Pool = Amazon price − our landed − Amazon fees (referral + FBA).
Transfer price = landed + half pool. Both sides earn the same $/bag.

**Communication rule: NEVER say "50/50" to the distributor.** The ratio
invites misreading as "half of retail" ($14.00 — one cent above our landed
cost). Quote ONE NUMBER only.

Working numbers (FBA fees approximate; confirm in Amazon revenue calculator
before locking):

| Amazon price | Amazon cut ~ | Pool | Each side | Transfer price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $27.99 | ~$10.50 | ~$3.50 | ~$1.75 | ~$15.75 |
| $32.99 | ~$11.50 | ~$7.50 | ~$3.75 | **~$17.75** |
| $34.99 | ~$12.00 | ~$9.00 | ~$4.50 | ~$18.50 |

At $27.99 the pool is too thin for anyone; Amazon channel should list at
$32.99+ (premium single-origin, direct-from-farm, Prime).

Terms that protect Tata's capital:
- Payment on transfer of inventory (not consignment) — investment recovered
  at handoff, every bag sold above $13.99 from the first sale.
- Fixed price per batch; recalculated per batch from actual landed + actual
  Amazon fee statements. The formula is the contract; the dollar figure floats.
- Each side keeps 100% of its own efficiencies: we cut landed (accurate DHL,
  larger batches) without sharing; they cut Amazon costs (FBM, size tiers)
  without sharing. No renegotiation triggered by either side improving.

## Failure modes and resolutions (agreed framing)

1. Unsold/stale inventory → start small (12–24 bags), roast dates on bags,
   optional 90-day buy-back at cost.
2. Buy Box / channel conflict → Tata's entity owns the ASIN; one authorized
   reseller; decide web-vs-Amazon price posture up front.
3. Amazon fee drift → recompute transfer price quarterly from fee statements;
   the split never moves, the number does.
4. Returns/damage → losses inside Amazon's system come out of the pool
   (shared); losses before Amazon handoff belong to owner-at-the-time.
5. Supply cost spikes → transfer price resets per batch; never fix a price
   across batches until the supplier's quote-accuracy commitment proves out.
6. Account/listing risk → HARD GATE: no listing before USPTO filing + Brand
   Registry under Tata Oro Productos LLC. Friends sell as authorized
   resellers on our ASIN.
7. Relationship ambiguity → one signed page, bilingual: price per batch,
   payment on transfer, buy-back, ASIN ownership, 30-day exit.

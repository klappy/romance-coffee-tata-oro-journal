# Session — Instagram Poll Results & Stories Sequence (2026-05-27)

> Instagram poll returned ~78/22 pre-ground/whole-bean split. Ground-only launch confirmed by audience data. Four-beat Stories sequence drafted for poll close-out. Couple-together content identified as outperforming solo content.

## Observations

- **Poll results:** 159 votes from 1,488 views. First reading 67/33, final ~78/22 pre-ground/whole-bean. 10.7% vote rate.
- **Poll question designed for behavior, not preference:** "When you make coffee at home, what do you actually use?" with parenthetical explanations to prevent aspirational answers.
- **Couple-together content outperforms:** Stories and content featuring Chris and Tatiana together around coffee generate higher engagement than Tatiana's solo hair care content. The couple dynamic IS the brand story and the audience rewards it.
- **Degassing valve consideration flagged** for ground coffee bags to preserve freshness.

## Decisions

- Ground-only launch confirmed by audience data (~78% pre-ground)
- Whole-bean buyers honored with forward promise but not served in first run
- (Later revised June 2 to 50/50 split for personal consumption + shelf-life reasons)

## Learnings

- Polls on Instagram Stories serve triple function: collect data, create pre-launch awareness, generate social proof
- Poll questions about behavior produce truer data than questions about preference
- Tatiana's audience is fashion-and-beauty-leaning, not coffee-enthusiast — confirms "everyday people" positioning

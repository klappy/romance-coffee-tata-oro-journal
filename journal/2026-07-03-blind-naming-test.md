# Blind Naming Test — Romance Coffee by Tata Oro (2026-07-03)

> A blind consumer panel scored five candidate social handles + brand phrases on memorability, clarity, purchase intent, trust transfer from Tata Oro, and searchability. Two personas were run separately and blind: the Spanish-dominant Tata Oro follower (the real buyer) and a cold English-speaking coffee buyer who has never heard of Tata Oro. Sixty simulated evaluators (six per persona per name). **Primary-buyer winner: `@cafetataoro` / "Café Tata Oro" (6.03/10)** — but it collides with the ratified brand-name lock. **Captain's gut pick "Romance Café" finished last with the actual buyer (3.87)**, its trust-transfer collapsing to 1.33 because it severs the Tata Oro tie. Recommended, canon-compatible identity: **`@romancebytataoro`**. Naming ruling remains the captain's; audience-facing usage goes through Tatiana's review.

## Method

- **Design:** blind panel via 10 Cowork native subagents (2 personas × 5 names), each subagent simulating **6 distinct in-persona evaluators** — 60 evaluators total, 6 per persona per name (exceeds the ≥5 minimum). Each subagent saw exactly **one** handle + brand phrase and was blind to the other four candidates, to the captain's preferences, and to all project deliberations. This gives every name an independent, uncontaminated panel.
- **Stimulus control:** each mock presented `handle` + display `Name` + an **identical neutral bio** ("Colombian specialty coffee ☕ Roasted with love · 12 oz · ships from Florida"), so the only variable under test is the handle + brand phrase.
- **Scoring:** each evaluator scored five dimensions 1–10 and wrote a verbatim comment. Evaluators were instructed to be skeptical and use the full range (LLM sycophancy guard).
- **Blind-but-weighed considerations** (hidden from evaluators, applied only in analysis): "café" = *coffee* in Spanish but *coffee-shop* in English; the ~200k Spanish-dominant IG audience is the primary funnel; cross-platform handle consistency (IG/TikTok/FB) is required; and the brand name is **locked in canon as "Romance by Tata Oro" — explicitly not "Tata Oro Cafe."**
- **Limitation:** evaluators are LLM-simulated personas, not recruited humans — directional signal, not survey-grade measurement. Within-name panels share one model call, so treat cross-name gaps larger than the reported spread as meaningful and smaller ones as noise.

## Ranked recommendation (evidence → routing)

1. **Empirical winner with the buyer is `@cafetataoro` / "Café Tata Oro"** (6.03; funnel-composite 5.23). It leads on the three practical axes at once: memorability (7.17), trust transfer (5.83), searchability (5.83). In Spanish "café" *is* coffee — instant clarity — and the short, name-matching handle is easy to remember and type. **But it collides head-on with a ratified canon lock** (brand name = "Romance by Tata Oro", explicitly *not* "Tata Oro Cafe"; gold cursive "Romance" script non-negotiable). The data favors an identity canon has already ruled out. **This tension is the captain's to resolve** — do not treat the test as overturning the lock.

2. **Strongest canon-compatible option: `@romancebytataoro` / "Romance by Tata Oro"** — #2 with the buyer (4.8), it preserves the locked brand name and carries the trust engine ("Tata Oro" present, trust 5.5). Its gap to Café Tata Oro is driven by two recoverable factors: Spanish-language clarity of "café" (recoverable via the bio/first-frame "café colombiano") and handle length (a fixed cost). **This is the recommended default if the lock stands.**

3. **Reject "Romance Café" (`@romancecafe`) as the primary funnel identity.** It finished **last** with the real buyer (3.87) and its trust transfer **collapsed to 1.33** — evaluators did not connect it to anyone they follow ("no la relaciono con nadie que yo siga"). It only looked good to the cold English audience, who will not convert. It optimizes for non-buyers by severing the single most valuable asset.

4. **Middle: `@romance.tataoro` (4.63)** and **`@romancecafetataoro` (4.27)**. The compound handle was punished hardest on searchability by the buyer (2.83) — "jamás lo voy a poder escribir." The dotted variant read as possibly-fake to several ("me suena raro, como cuenta fake").

5. **Cross-cutting fix, independent of the name:** every Tata-Oro-bearing option drew the same doubt — *"no sé si es ella misma o alguien usando su nombre."* That is a **verification problem, not a naming problem.** Whichever handle wins, launch with Tatiana's face, her own voice announcing the brand, and a platform verification cue — the trust transfer is gated on confirmed authorship.

## Decisions

1. **[D] Recommendation (recommended, not ruled — the naming ruling is the captain's):** for the social handle + display identity, the strongest canon-compatible option is **`@romancebytataoro` / "Romance by Tata Oro"** — #2 with the primary buyer (4.8), preserving the locked brand name and the trust engine. Reject "Romance Café" as the primary funnel identity: it optimizes for non-buyers and severs the Tata Oro trust link. If the captain chooses to reopen the brand-name lock in light of "Café Tata Oro" testing first with the buyer, that is a captain + Tatiana decision, not this test's to make.

## Observations

2. **[O]** The blind panel (60 evaluators, blind to alternatives and to all deliberations) ranked **"Café Tata Oro" #1 with the primary buyer (6.03)** and #1 on the funnel-weighted composite (5.23), leading on memorability, trust transfer, and searchability. **Captain's gut pick "Romance Café" ranked last with the buyer (3.87)**, trust transfer collapsing to 1.33.
3. **[O]** The cold English buyer is **nearly insensitive to the Tata Oro tie** — trust floored ~1.5–2.0 across all five names, overall scores in a dead heat (3.87–4.20, within one stdev). "Romance Café" nominally topped this persona only on English coffee-shop charm; this audience does not convert, so optimizing the name for it sacrifices the funnel that does.

## Learnings

4. **[L]** **"Tata Oro" in the handle is the trust engine** for the ~200k primary audience. Every name carrying it scored trust 4.2–5.8 with the buyer; the one name that drops it ("Romance Café") is the only one whose trust craters (1.33). Removing "Tata Oro" severs the most valuable brand asset even though it tests best with people who will never buy.
5. **[L]** A **pervasive authenticity doubt** appeared across all Tata-Oro-bearing names — "no sé si es ella misma o alguien usando su nombre." This is a **verification problem, not a naming problem**: solved by Tatiana's face/voice + a verification cue at launch, not by name choice.
6. **[L]** **"Romance" reduces clarity for the primary audience**, who know Tatiana for hair and faith and read "romance + coffee" as a double cognitive jump ("pensé que era algo de amor o una cafetería romántica"). Long compound handles (`@romancecafetataoro`) were punished hardest on searchability by the buyer (2.83).
7. **[L] Debrief (black box → canon):** the first officer's original handle slate **omitted "Romance Café" without evidence** — an untested naming preference presented as if vetted, which the captain caught on cross-check. Corrective rule reaffirmed: candidate names are enumerated and tested, or explicitly ruled out with a stated reason, before any slate is presented as decision-ready.

## Constraints / Tension

8. **[C/Tension]** The **empirical winner "Café Tata Oro" collides with a ratified canon lock**: `brand-constraints.md` locks the brand name as "Romance by Tata Oro" and explicitly states "not Tata Oro Cafe," with the gold cursive "Romance" script locked and non-negotiable. Constraint restated explicitly: the packaged/logo brand name **must not** become "Café Tata Oro" or "Tata Oro Cafe" without the captain (and Tatiana, brand-feel veto) re-clearing the visual-identity lock. The social handle is a *distinct* surface from the logo lockup, but adopting a handle that contradicts the brand name **must** be a conscious, ratified choice — not a silent consequence of this test.

## Handoffs

9. **[H]** The **naming ruling remains the captain's**. Escalate the "Café Tata Oro"-versus-brand-lock tension to the captain and to Tatiana (brand-feel veto). **All audience-facing usage** of the chosen handle/bio goes through **Tatiana's authorial-voice review** before publishing.

## Opens

10. **[?] (P2)** Handle **availability is unverifiable until live signup**; cross-platform consistency (IG/TikTok/FB) is required, so the winning handle and its fallback must be claimable on all three simultaneously.
11. **[?] (P2)** If the captain leans toward the buyer-preferred "café" signal while honoring the "Romance by Tata Oro" lock, open a follow-up: can bio/first-frame copy ("café colombiano") recover the clarity gap without touching the locked name?

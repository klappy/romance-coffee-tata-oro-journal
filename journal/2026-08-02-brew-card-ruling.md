# Session — Brew Card Ruling (2026-08-02)

> Captain ruling, recorded from the 2026-08-02 dispatch session. The story is what sells and it comes first — but not on the bag. The bag stays clean and simple. The in-bag insert is a **brew card**: bilingual EN/ES brewing instructions and simple tips. Layout question (both languages on one side vs. one language per side) is open for review against the drafted copy.

## Decisions

1. **[D] The story sells — and it leads, but never on the bag.** The story remains the primary selling asset and comes first on the surfaces built to carry it (insert card, `cafe.tataoro.com`, social). The bag itself stays clean and minimal, per the designer's ratified minimalism and the 2026-07-03 "don't fight the bag" learning. No pressure to move story content back onto the bag.
2. **[D] The insert is the BREW CARD.** Something simple, printed in-house: brewing instructions and informative, simple tips for making the coffee. This sharpens the 2026-07-03 insert-card decision — the card's job is instructional first; story and QR ride along per the earlier ruling.
3. **[D] The brew card is bilingual EN/ES.** Both languages ship on every card.

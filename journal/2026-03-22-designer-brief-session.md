# Session — Designer Brief & Packaging Direction (2026-03-22)

> Created 16-section bilingual designer brief for Romance packaging. Core illustration concept: coffee steam transforms into embracing couple silhouettes. Negative space implies a heart — no literal heart graphic.

## Observations

- **Illustration concept locked:** Steam-to-embracing-couple transformation — romantic without cliché. "Romance sin caer en lo cliché."
- **Bag style:** Matte black, strong negative space, minimal design
- **Typography:** Georgia serif throughout, gold accent dividers
- **Color palette:** Black, gold, warm gray — mirrors Tata Oro DNA
- **Deliverable:** 16-section brief formatted as polished .docx, sent to designer. 3–5 concepts requested.

## Constraints

- No literal heart graphics — implied through negative space only
- Couple must be recognizable as Chris and Tatiana (specific physical details noted)
- Must respect existing Tata Oro visual DNA while standing distinct as coffee brand
- "Romance by Tata Oro" cursive gold script is locked and non-negotiable

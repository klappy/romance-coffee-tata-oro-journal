# Session — Insert Card, Website Conversion Hub, Brand Guide (2026-07-03)

> The designer cut the tasting notes and deeper story from the bag. Recovery: an in-house color-laser-printed insert card in every bag carries the story, the tasting notes and how they work, and a QR to cafe.tataoro.com. The website becomes the conversion engine — everything routes back to it for single-click purchase. A Brand Guide was created as the design source of truth to seed a landing-page bakeoff.

## Decisions

1. **[D] Insert card recovers the cut content.** The designer refused the tasting notes and the fuller story on the bag. Recovery: a color-laser-printed (in-house) insert card slipped into every bag carrying (a) the story, (b) the tasting notes and how they work with the blend, and (c) a QR code to `cafe.tataoro.com`. On-demand printing lets card copy iterate without reprinting bags.
2. **[D] `cafe.tataoro.com` is the conversion engine.** Every channel (bag QR, card QR, social) routes back to the site; the site must deliver the final conversion via **single-click purchase**. Landing-page job: traffic → story → single-click buy.
3. **[D] Brand Guide created** at `canon/governance/brand-guide.md` as the design/web source of truth (palette sampled from the label, typography, voice, imagery, packaging system, landing-page blueprint, bakeoff brief). `brand-constraints.md` remains binding; the guide operationalizes it.

## Learnings

4. **[L] Don't fight the bag — own the card and the web.** On-bag real estate and the designer's minimalism are hard limits. The content the captain wanted is recovered on surfaces we fully control (in-house card + website), which also lets it iterate cheaply.

## Constraints

5. **[C] Tatiana review gate.** Card copy (story + tasting notes) and all website copy carry Tatiana's authorial-voice and brand-feel review before publishing.

## Handoffs

6. **[H] Bakeoff.** Build competing landing-page concepts against the brand guide; goal = drive traffic + single-click sales; judged on brand/story fit and conversion. Scope (concept count, coded prototypes vs. specs, checkout mechanism) to be confirmed with the captain before building.

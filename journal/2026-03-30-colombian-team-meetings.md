# Session — Colombian Design Team Meetings & Brand Architecture (2026-03-30)

> Two meetings with Colombian design/marketing team. Brand architecture confirmed: Tata Oro umbrella, Romance as coffee brand. Brand book received. 45-day manufacturing bottleneck identified. Follow-up email drafted for Elkin. Key learnings on Stories conversion and Colombian business culture.

## Observations

- Meeting held with Andrea, Elkin, Gustavo, Laura (Colombian design/marketing team)
- Brand book delivered by Tatiana's designer — isotipo (couple line art in circle), cursive gold "Romance" script, color palette (Irish Coffee, Butterscotch Mousse, Cocoa Cream, Gold), two bag mockup directions
- Tatiana's Instagram stories: ~11K engagement per story; reels reaching 4x in 24 hours
- Conversion benchmark: 1–3% on $9.90 eBook from 5K views = $500–$1,500
- Coffee samples delayed by Colombian Semana Santa + World of Coffee expo in San Diego
- Chris and Tatiana mid-residential move — delivery address confirmed at 2546 Winter Sky St, Kissimmee FL 34744

## Decisions

1. **LOCKED:** Brand name is "Romance by Tata Oro" (not "Tata Oro Café")
2. **LOCKED:** Cursive gold script for "Romance" stays as-is — non-negotiable
3. **LOCKED:** Gold-on-black color DNA carries from Tata Oro parent brand
4. **LOCKED:** First run is 100 bags with stickers, not 1,000 with custom printing
5. **DECIDED:** Unified bag design + variant stickers per coffee type
6. **OPEN:** Isotipo design — flexible, team can propose alternatives
7. **OPEN:** Final bag direction (minimal vs botanical illustration)
8. **OPEN:** Coffee variant selection — dependent on sample tasting

## Learnings

- Stories convert better than reels for Tata's audience — "that's where the people actually buy"
- Colombian business communication requires relational warmth and directness without commanding tone
- Elkin is also a pastor — tone must honor that relationship
- Timeline language matters: "end of May" communicates urgency; "before June" sounds like a full month of buffer
- Never dictate or command — frame requests as shared goals

## Constraints

- 45-day manufacturing timeline AFTER coffee selection — critical path bottleneck
- Target launch: before mid-year (Tata's expectation communicated via text)
- Regulatory: US labeling requirements deferred to parallel track
- Colombian Spanish defaults in all communications

- Gustavo → Chris/Tata: First packaging concepts 2–3 days after brand manual
- Elkin → Chris: Coffee samples shipping notification (ETA post-Semana Santa)
- Chris → Elkin: Follow-up email drafted and sent

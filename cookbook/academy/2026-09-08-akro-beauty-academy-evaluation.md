# Akro Beauty Academy — Tata Oro channel evaluation

## Context

Tata Oro has been invited to create courses for sale through Akro Beauty Academy, aimed at both consumers and professional hair stylists.

This is an initial channel/platform evaluation. It is not a commitment to Akro and does not establish verified commercial terms.

## Current recommendation

Pursue the invitation as a channel experiment, but do not make Akro Tata Oro's canonical education home or hand over finished course assets until the commercial, IP, data, and exit terms are verified.

The strategic opportunity is strong because education can create a learn → trust → use → replenish loop around Tata Oro products. Professional education may also create a stylist-acquisition and recurring product-demand channel.

## Potential advantages

- Existing beauty-education context rather than building an audience and LMS from zero.
- Potential access to an existing stylist/customer audience.
- Courses can generate direct digital revenue while also teaching correct Tata Oro product use.
- Professional courses can position Tata Oro as a technical/educational brand rather than only a product seller.
- Consumer courses can improve confidence, outcomes, product selection, and kit attachment.
- A platform partner may absorb hosting, accounts, video delivery, checkout, learner progress, and other LMS operations.
- Useful low-capital way to test demand before Tata Oro invests in its own academy infrastructure.

## Potential disadvantages / risks

The primary risk is not LMS functionality; it is loss of the compounding asset Tata Oro creates.

Verify before commitment:

- course and curriculum IP ownership;
- ownership/license of video recordings and derivative materials;
- instructor name, image, likeness, and Tata Oro trademark rights;
- exclusivity or restrictions on publishing the same education elsewhere;
- pricing and discount authority;
- revenue share, fees, payout timing, refunds, chargebacks, and taxes;
- customer/student data ownership and exportability;
- ability for Tata Oro to communicate and remarket to its students;
- analytics available to Tata Oro;
- responsibility for learner support;
- course portability and access for existing students after termination;
- termination rights and post-termination content use;
- whether Akro may continue marketing with Tata Oro assets after the relationship ends.

Red flags include exclusive course rights, Akro ownership of Tata Oro course material, perpetual broad content licenses, inability to export useful student/customer data, unilateral discounting, restrictions on Tata Oro selling education elsewhere, indefinite trademark/likeness rights, opaque revenue accounting, or no practical exit path.

## Product strategy hypothesis

Treat consumer and professional education as separate ladders.

### Consumer

Results-focused education tied directly to product usage and bundles: repair, curls, moisture, home treatment, correct application, routines, and product selection.

### Professional stylist

Technique and service education: treatment protocols, consultation/diagnosis, service construction, product selection/application, and potentially certification or continuing education later if justified.

## Due diligence still owed

1. Identify the legal/operator entity behind Akro Beauty Academy.
2. Identify Gathered and its actual relationship to Akro.
3. Determine the underlying LMS/commerce technology and vendor dependencies.
4. Review current catalog, instructors, course pricing, merchandising, and learner UX.
5. Walk signup, checkout, payment, course access, and account flows.
6. Locate and review terms, privacy policy, instructor/creator agreement, refund policy, and IP provisions.
7. Determine actual revenue split and all platform/payment fees.
8. Determine customer/student data access, portability, analytics, and marketing permissions.
9. Assess Akro's real distribution value: audience, traffic, stylist reach, promotion commitments, and discovery surfaces.
10. Score the opportunity for Tata Oro as go / negotiate / no-go based on economics, audience value, ownership, portability, and strategic leverage.

## Decision principle

Akro is most attractive as a distribution and demand-validation channel while Tata Oro retains the durable assets: curriculum, media, brand, instructor reputation, audience relationship, customer insight, and the ability to publish elsewhere.

## Ground Coffee Shelf Life (Evidence-Based Guidance)

Specialty coffee ground loses peak freshness faster than whole bean because grinding increases surface area and exposes oils/aromatics to oxygen, light, and moisture.  

**Observed data (National Coffee Association + specialty storage research):**  
- **Ground coffee** (room temperature, airtight container): Peak freshness window **1–2 weeks**. After that it remains safe to drink but loses vibrant aroma, flavor clarity, and mouthfeel.  
- **Whole bean** (room temperature, airtight/one-way valve bag): Optimal **2–4 weeks** post-roast; can stretch to several months if stored cool, dark, and sealed. Freezing (in airtight portions) extends to 3–6+ months with minimal quality loss.  

**Practical plan for your 100-bag buffer:** Use the ground half first (or portion into smaller airtight containers / freeze in vacuum bags and thaw as needed). Keep the whole-bean half for longer-term storage and for your own daily ritual grinding — exactly as you have been doing with the fresh cinnamon bark. The 50/50 split protects quality while meeting audience convenience.

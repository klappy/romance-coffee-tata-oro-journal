# Constraint — Internal Success First (2026-05-29)

**Type:** Constraint  
**Quality:** Strong

## Constraint
The product succeeds the moment the first bag delivers the exact chocolate + cinnamon ritual coffee to Tatiana every morning. External sales, while welcome, are secondary. The 100-bag personal buffer guarantees this outcome regardless of outside demand.

This constraint protects the heart of the project (the living “algún día” promise kept) from being commodified or rushed for volume.

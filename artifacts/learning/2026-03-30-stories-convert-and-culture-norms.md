# Learning — Stories Convert Better & Colombian Business Culture Norms (2026-03-30)

> Two key learnings from the March 30 meetings. First: Tatiana's Instagram Stories convert to sales better than reels — "that's where the people actually buy." Second: Colombian business communication requires relational warmth and directness without commanding tone. Communications with Elkin (also a pastor) need to say "end of May" not "before June" (the latter implies a full month of buffer).

**Type:** Learning
**Quality:** Strong

## Stories vs Reels

- Stories are where Tatiana's audience actually buys — higher conversion despite lower total reach
- Reels drive awareness (4x reach in 24 hours); Stories drive revenue
- This informs launch content strategy: launch announcement through Stories, not just reels

## Colombian Business Culture

- Communication style should be direct but relationally warm
- Tatiana observed the Colombian team's communication is sometimes indirect — Chris needs to balance directness with respect
- Elkin is also a pastor — tone must honor that relationship
- Timeline language matters: "end of May" communicates urgency; "before June" sounds like a full month of buffer
- Never dictate or command — frame requests as shared goals ("We need to make sure we understand the timeline")

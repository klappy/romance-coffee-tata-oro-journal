# Learning — Panela Vocabulary Correction (2026-05-26)

> "Piniella" was a phonetic invention from a voice transcript. "Piloncillo" is Mexican Spanish. The correct Colombian word — used by both Tatiana and the supplier — is "panela." The error survived three encoding passes before being caught. Colombian Spanish vocabulary takes precedence in all Romance by Tata Oro communications.

**Type:** Learning
**Quality:** Strong

## The Error Chain

1. May 16 transcript: phonetic guess "Piniella" committed to journal
2. Gauntlet pass: preserved unchallenged
3. Brief draft: carried forward as "piloncillo" (Mexican Spanish correction that was still wrong for the audience)
4. Tatiana's review: caught and corrected to "panela"

## The Principle

- Colombian Spanish vocabulary takes precedence over generic or Mexican Spanish
- When a Spanish word in transcripts is not immediately recognizable, look it up before encoding
- Vocabulary verification should be a separate audit pass, not assumed to be caught in content review
- Writing to Colombians using non-Colombian Spanish signals the writer has not internalized the audience's voice

## Constraint

All Romance by Tata Oro communications use Colombian Spanish as the default. This is a partnership-respect constraint.

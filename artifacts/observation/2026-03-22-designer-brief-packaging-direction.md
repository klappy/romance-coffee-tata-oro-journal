# Observation — Designer Brief & Packaging Direction (2026-03-22)

> 16-section designer brief created for Romance packaging. Core illustration concept: coffee steam rising from a cup transforms into the silhouettes of an embracing couple. Negative space between the figures implies a heart — no literal heart graphic. The couple represents Chris and Tatiana with specific physical details noted for the illustrator.

**Type:** Observation
**Quality:** Strong
**Source:** Bilingual designer brief document (translated and formatted as .docx)

## Key Packaging Direction

- **Illustration concept:** Steam-to-embracing-couple transformation — romantic without being cliché
- **Critical directive:** "Romance sin caer en lo cliché" (Romance without falling into cliché)
- **Bag style:** Matte black, strong negative space, minimal design
- **Typography:** Georgia serif throughout, gold accent dividers
- **Color palette:** Black, gold, warm gray — mirrors brand DNA
- **Deliverable to designer:** 3–5 concepts requested
- **Format:** 16 structured sections for easy scanning

## Design Constraints Named

- No literal heart graphics — implied through negative space only
- Couple must be recognizable as Chris and Tatiana (not generic stock figures)
- Must respect existing Tata Oro visual DNA while standing distinct as a coffee brand
- "Romance by Tata Oro" cursive gold script is locked and non-negotiable

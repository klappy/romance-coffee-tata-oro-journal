# Observation — Instagram Poll Results (2026-05-27)

> Tatiana ran an Instagram Story poll asking her audience about actual brewing behavior (not preference). First reading: 159 votes from 1,488 views, 67/33 split favoring pre-ground. Later consolidated to ~78/22 pre-ground/whole-bean. Poll question designed to capture behavior, not aspiration: "When you make coffee at home, what do you actually use?"

**Type:** Observation
**Quality:** Strong
**Source:** Instagram Story poll data from Tatiana's account

## Poll Design

- Question framed around behavior, not preference — "What do you actually use?" not "What do you prefer?"
- Parenthetical explanations added to make it embarrassing to answer aspirationally:
  - "Pre-ground (out of the bag, into the pot)"
  - "Whole bean (I grind it myself)"
- Framed as launch teaser: "Helping my husband make a decision for our coffee launch"

## Results

- **First reading:** 159 votes / 1,488 views — 67% pre-ground / 33% whole bean
- **Final reading:** ~78% pre-ground / ~22% whole bean
- 10.7% vote rate (159/1,488) — strong engagement for a poll

## What the Poll Revealed

- Tatiana's audience is mostly drip-pot drinkers — confirms "everyday people" positioning
- Validates single-SKU ground launch as the primary format
- The 22% whole-bean segment is real but minority — worth honoring with a forward promise, not splitting initial inventory
- Degassing valve consideration flagged for ground coffee bags to preserve freshness

## Triple Function of the Poll

1. Collected real data on inventory split
2. Created pre-launch awareness (every voter now knows a coffee is coming)
3. Generated social proof — "Tatiana's audience is asking" is a different sentence than "Tatiana's audience is ready"

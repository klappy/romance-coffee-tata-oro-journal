# Decision — Ground-Only Launch Confirmed by Audience Data (2026-05-27)

> Instagram poll (~78/22 pre-ground/whole-bean) validates single-SKU ground format for launch. Whole-bean buyers explicitly honored with a forward promise but not served in the first production run. This is a prioritization, not an exclusion.

**Type:** Decision
**Quality:** Strong

## Decision

Launch with ground coffee only as a single SKU. The poll data resolves what was previously a gut-feel decision into a data-backed one.

## Rationale

- ~78% of Tatiana's audience uses pre-ground — the majority is clear
- Splitting 50/50 over-serves a 22% minority and creates operational complexity at 100-bag scale
- The audience is fashion-and-beauty-leaning, not coffee-enthusiast-leaning
- Whole bean can be offered in a second run if demand warrants it

## Note

This decision was later revised on 2026-06-02 to a 50/50 ground/whole-bean split after considering personal consumption needs (whole bean stays fresher for the ritual grinding) and the practical shelf-life concerns of ground specialty coffee. See `artifacts/decision/2026-06-02-50-50-split-and-cc-payment.md`.
